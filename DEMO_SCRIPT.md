# Demo Script

1. Start the FastAPI server.
2. Open the web interface.
3. Ask:
   "Hi, I am looking for a 3 BHK."
4. Ask:
   "My budget is around 1.5 crore."
5. Show that Gemini remembers the configuration and budget.
6. Ask:
   "What amenities do you have?"
7. Show that the AI refuses to invent unavailable facts.
8. Ask:
   "I want a site visit on 2026-09-10 at 11:00 AM."
9. Show the simulated booking result.
10. Open the analytics panel.
11. Show configuration, budget, interest and site-visit status.
12. Reset and demonstrate the opt-out flow if time permits.
